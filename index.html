Sept 21–25 · In class

Complete the prescreen

Your professor will set aside up to 10 minutes during one of your classes to do it together. It is a short survey of basic demographics in 7 short sections, and you tap Record Responses at the end of each. Plan to finish in one sitting, and answer honestly, since that is how studies get matched to you. If you would rather not wait, complete it on your own in SONA before then. If you finish early, you are all set and can sit out the in-class session.

  • Any device works for the prescreen: laptop, tablet, or phone. On a phone, the login box sits below the notices at the top, so scroll down to User ID. Later studies need a laptop or desktop.
  • Finish both steps by Fri Sept 25 and you are guaranteed enough study opportunities to earn full credit.

Those three steps unlock everything. No study invitations arrive until you have logged in and finished prescreening.

How invitations workWhy they arrive when they do
  • Studies are invitation-only, and your unique access code unlocks each one.
  • Capacity is limited, with about 1,500 students in the program, so invitations go out in rotating batches.
  • If you are not invited to a particular study, more opportunities follow.

Online invitations

Your spot is guaranteed. Sign up and complete the study within 3 days. If the window passes, the spot goes to the next group.

In-person invitations

These go to all eligible students at once. You are booking a specific appointment slot, usually 1–2 weeks out, so pick any time that works for you.

What affects how often you are invited

  • Setup finished: both login and prescreening are done
  • Study fit: your prescreening answers match what researchers need, so please answer honestly
  • Availability: how many studies are running that week
  • Track record: consistent attendance

No invitation yet? That is expected, and there is nothing you need to do. Questions about studies come to the Lab rather than your professor, since we hold the schedule.

Rules and cancelingHow to take part, and what to do if plans change

While taking a study

  • Please do not discuss study content with others, since it can bias their responses
  • Answer thoughtfully and honestly
  • Find a quiet space with few distractions for online studies
  • Read the instructions before you begin, and give yourself enough time
  • Watch for attention-check questions

Canceling

Plans change, and canceling properly is always better than missing a session.

  • In person: cancel at least 6 hours ahead, by replying to your confirmation email or in SONA
  • Online: if you cannot finish within the 3-day window, reply to the invitation email so we can reassign your spot

Excused

  • Canceled within the required timeframe
  • Told us the reason, such as illness, an emergency, or a technical issue

Unexcused

  • No-show without notice
  • Late cancellation without a reason
  • Repeated missed commitments

Unexcused absences deprioritize you for that study, and possibly for later ones, so a make-up is not guaranteed. If something comes up, contact us as early as you can and we will do what we can to help.

Why this matters for 497The reasoning behind the requirement

Strategy runs on behavior

BUAD 497 treats strategy as decisions made under uncertainty. Those decisions hinge on how people and organizations actually behave, which is what these studies examine.

You weigh real evidence

The course expects you to assess the evidence behind strategy concepts rather than take it at face value. Taking part shows you how that evidence is produced.

Research from the inside

Most students only read about studies. Here you take part in one, and your responses feed research that reaches publication and practice.

Participation is voluntary, and you may withdraw from any study at any time without affecting your grade or standing. Responses stay confidential, are never linked to your identity, and every study is approved by the USC Institutional Review Board.

FAQ

Account and login problemsWhich email has my login, no email, new accounts, cannot log in

Which email has my login?

Not the welcome email from Brian Huh. Your login comes in a separate automated email from the SONA platform (marshall-mor-admin@sona-systems.net), usually within a few hours of the welcome email. It has your User ID, which is your NetID, and your Identity Code. It does not include a password.

No SONA email yet?

Emails go out by class section as rosters are processed, so timing varies a little. It comes from outside USC, so search your inbox and spam for sona-systems first. If it still has not arrived within a few hours of the welcome email, email mor.sona@marshall.usc.edu and we will follow up.

Never took BUAD 304?

You have no account to reactivate. Email mor.sona@marshall.usc.edu and we will set one up for you, or go to marshall-mor.sona-systems.com, scroll to the bottom of the page, and click Request Account. The red notice there about not creating a new account is for returning students. Then complete the prescreen.

Cannot log in?

Log in with your NetID and the password you set in BUAD 304. Do not remember it? Click Forgot Password at the bottom of the login page and SONA emails you a reset link, so check spam. Still not working? Email mor.sona@marshall.usc.edu.

User ID not working?

Use your NetID only, such as smithj, not smithj@usc.edu.

Prescreening will not save?

It has 7 short sections, and you record each one with the Record Responses button at the end of the section. Plan to finish in one sitting. If it will not save, email mor.sona@marshall.usc.edu and tell us what you saw.

Invitations and sessionsNothing arriving, missed one, or missed a session

No study invitations yet?

They begin only after you log in and finish prescreening, since both are required. After that they roll out in batches.

Missed an invitation?

Your priority for that study moves to the end of the semester, so another chance is not guaranteed. If you know you cannot commit, reply to the invitation to cancel before the 3-day window closes.

Missed an in-person session?

Contact us as soon as you can. If spots are available, we may be able to reschedule you.

Credits and gradesHow credits count, how many studies, missing credits

How does this affect my grade?

Your instructor sets how participation credits become points, so check your syllabus or ask them directly.

How many studies will I need?

Usually 3 to 6, depending on length. As long as setup is done on time, there will be enough studies to reach the full 2.0 credits.

Credits not showing up?

They usually post within 24 hours. If it has been longer, email us the study name and completion date and we will look into it.
